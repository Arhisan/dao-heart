Privacy Policy for Dao Heart

Effective October 8, 2026.

In short

Dao Heart keeps your game progress and settings only locally on your device. Through gameplay, the developer does not receive the contents of your save, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

AppMetrica (Yandex) is used for product analytics. It receives gameplay and technical events needed to understand how the game is used and to analyze its balance and stability. You can turn analytics off in the game settings.

The Google Play and RuStore versions may show rewarded ads through the Yandex Advertising Network and Google AdMob. The ad SDKs may themselves receive the IP address, the advertising ID and technical information about the device. The Huawei AppGallery version does not use ad SDKs.

1. Developer and data controller

Dao Heart (Russian title «Сердце Дао», Android package app.daoheart.game) is published under the name JAT Global Mobile.

For questions about privacy, data processing or exercising your rights, you can contact the developer at:

jat.global.mobile@gmail.com

The data controller is the developer of the game, a private individual from Russia acting under the name above. The controller’s full name and location are provided on request at jat.global.mobile@gmail.com.

2. What is stored on your device

The app’s internal storage may hold:

  • game and interface settings;
  • game progress, achievements and in-game resources;
  • the state of the current or an unfinished run;
  • local technical data needed for compatibility and for restoring the save.

This information is used to run the game and keep your progress. The save file itself is not sent to the developer.

In normal operation, Android keeps the save in the app’s internal storage. The operating system may include it in a device or account backup if you have turned that feature on.

The game has no user accounts, chat or leaderboards. It does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, contacts or precise location for gameplay.

3. Analytics

Product and technical analytics are provided by AppMetrica, a service of YANDEX LLC.

The game may send the following categories of events to AppMetrica:

  • the start and end of play sessions;
  • the selected mode and stage of game progress;
  • the result, duration and main parameters of a run;
  • gameplay actions and choices needed to analyze balance and progression;
  • achievements and in-game rewards received;
  • interactions with the offer to watch a rewarded ad;
  • technical errors and information needed to diagnose crashes.

The game does not send AppMetrica your name, email address, phone number, the contents of your save, your precise location or the advertising ID. In the game’s AppMetrica configuration, the use of advertising identifiers and precise location detection are turned off.

As a technical service, AppMetrica may still receive the device’s IP address, the approximate location it derives from the IP address (country and city), technical parameters of the app and device and information about installs and sessions; it may also assign the install its own technical identifier. For this reason, this data is not considered fully anonymous.

Purpose of analytics

Analytics are used to:

  • find balance and difficulty problems;
  • understand how game modes and mechanics are used;
  • find technical errors and crashes;
  • improve the quality of the game.

The developer does not use AppMetrica data to build advertising profiles of players and does not sell it.

How to turn analytics off

You can turn off analytics in the game:

“Settings” → “All settings” → “Stats” → “Send statistics”.

Once it is off, the game sends no new analytics events. The ad SDKs and their data processing are governed separately and are not turned off by this switch.

4. Ads

This section applies to the Google Play and RuStore versions of the game.

The Huawei AppGallery version does not use the Yandex Mobile Ads and Google Mobile Ads SDKs or Google’s consent management tool.

In the supported versions, the game may show rewarded ads. An ad video starts only after you act, by choosing the corresponding offer in the game.

Ads are served using:

  • Yandex Mobile Ads SDK / Yandex Advertising Network;
  • Google Mobile Ads / Google AdMob, connected through ad mediation.

The ad libraries may start before you actually watch an ad, and ad content may be loaded in advance. This means the ad services may receive technical data even if you end up not watching the video.

What data the ad services may receive

Depending on the device, region, OS settings and ad SDK configuration, the ad services may receive:

  • the advertising ID and other device or app identifiers;
  • the IP address and approximate region;
  • device model, Android version, language and network type;
  • app name and version, information about installs and launches;
  • information about ad impressions, taps and views;
  • technical information about how the ad libraries work and about crashes.

The game does not send the ad networks the contents of your local save, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, personal files or precise location.

The ad services process the data they receive under their own policies and terms.

5. Ad consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

In regions where this is required, the game uses Google’s consent management tool to obtain and store your choice about ads. To find out whether consent is required, the game contacts this Google tool at every launch, whatever the region; when it does, Google receives the device’s IP address and technical information about the device.

Until the necessary answer is given, ads may not be requested. Depending on your choice, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or not shown at all.

If the option is available to you, you can change your choice in:

“Settings” → “All settings” → “Ads” → “Ad consent”.

7. Recipients of data

Depending on the version of the game and the features used, data may be processed by the following companies:

  • YANDEX LLC — AppMetrica and the Yandex Advertising Network;
  • Google — Google AdMob, Google Mobile Ads and the consent management tool;
  • the app store you install the game from, for example Google Play, Huawei AppGallery or RuStore.

The developer does not sell players’ personal data.

8. International data processing

AppMetrica and the ad services may process data on servers located in different countries.

According to the respective providers, Yandex may process data in Russia and the EU, and Google in various countries around the world. For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, this means that data is also processed outside their region, including in Russia, for which no adequacy decision has been adopted.

The rules for such processing, including the applicable international data transfer mechanisms, are set out in the documents of the respective service providers.

9. Retention

  • The local save stays on the device until you reset your progress, clear the app’s data or uninstall the game, subject to any backup made by Android.
  • AppMetrica data is kept for as long as the developer has the game’s project in AppMetrica and is deleted together with the project under the rules of the AppMetrica service.
  • The ad services set the retention periods for the data they receive in their own policies and rules.
  • Emails sent to the developer may be kept as long as needed to handle the enquiry, comply with applicable requirements and protect the developer’s legitimate interests.

10. Data security

The developer takes reasonable technical measures to limit the amount of information collected and to protect the data transmitted.

In particular:

  • the game save is not sent to the developer’s server;
  • analytics data is sent to the AppMetrica service over a secure connection;
  • the developer’s access to analytics reports is limited to the service account in use;
  • gameplay events are limited to the information needed for product analysis and technical diagnostics.

The security of data that Yandex, Google and the app stores receive on their own is also governed by their own measures and documents.

11. Android backup

If you have turned on Android backup, the operating system may save a copy of the game’s local data and restore it after you reinstall the app or move to another device.

This backup is performed by the operating system and the provider of the account concerned. The developer has no access to the contents of this backup and does not control its deletion.

12. Managing and deleting data

You can:

  • turn off gameplay analytics in the game settings;
  • reset your game progress in the game;
  • delete local data in Android settings or uninstall the app;
  • change your ad preferences through the consent tool available in the game;
  • reset or delete the advertising ID using the operating system, if this option is available;
  • contact Yandex or Google directly about data that these companies have received and process on their own.

Analytics events already sent to AppMetrica are linked to a technical install identifier, not to a player’s name or email address. For this reason, the developer may have no technical means of telling from a user’s email which analytics records relate to them.

  • request access to, correction or deletion of your data;
  • restrict processing or object to it;

  • lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

To exercise your rights regarding data held by the developer, write to jat.global.mobile@gmail.com.

If your data is processed by Yandex, Google or an app store on its own, you may need to send the request directly to that company.
